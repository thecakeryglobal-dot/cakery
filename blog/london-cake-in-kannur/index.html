← Back to all stories

Introduction

Some cakes look good. The London cake looks good and keeps a secret. From the outside it is a dark, glossy chocolate cake. Cut into it and a soft, golden toffee centre spills out, warm-tasting, buttery and just a little salty against the chocolate.

The London-style chocolate and caramel cake took over social media feeds this year, and Kannur has been asking for it. Now you can get it fresh from The Cakery, baked in Kannur and delivered to your door.

What is a London cake?

The London cake is a chocolate cake built for people who think a chocolate cake can never be too rich. It pairs moist chocolate sponge and smooth chocolate layers with a toffee core hidden in the middle.

That contrast is the whole point:

  • Rich chocolate for depth and that slightly bitter cocoa finish.
  • A hidden toffee centre for sweetness, butteriness and a soft, gooey texture.
  • The reveal, because the first slice is always the best moment at any party.

It is the kind of cake people photograph before they eat it and then go back for a second slice.

London cake vs. a regular chocolate cake

A regular chocolate cake is all about the sponge and the frosting. It is soft, sweet and familiar, and every bite tastes more or less the same. The London cake keeps everything you love about a chocolate cake and adds a second texture right in the middle.

If you usually order a chocolate truffle cake, the London cake is the natural next step. It is just as rich, but the toffee keeps each bite interesting, from the first slice to the last.

Why is Kannur falling for it?

Kannur loves its celebrations, and a celebration needs a cake that stands out on the table. The London cake does three things well:

  • It surprises your guests. Nobody expects the toffee until the knife goes in.
  • It suits every age. Kids love the gooey centre; adults love the deep chocolate.
  • It works for any occasion. Birthdays, anniversaries, office parties, a Friday night treat, or simply because it has been a long week.

How to store and serve your London cake

A few simple steps help your London cake taste as good at the table as it did in our kitchen:

  1. Refrigerate it on arrival. Kannur's warm, humid weather softens chocolate quickly, so keep the cake in the fridge until you are close to serving it.
  2. Take it out 20 to 30 minutes before cutting. This lets the toffee centre soften and turn gooey again, and brings out the full chocolate flavour.
  3. Use a warm knife. Dip the knife in hot water and wipe it dry before each cut. You will get clean slices and a neat toffee reveal.
  4. Cut from the centre. Start your first slice through the middle so everyone sees the toffee core.
  5. Store leftovers covered. Put any remaining cake back in the fridge in a closed box, and enjoy it within a day or two for the best taste.

Want to make it extra special? Serve each slice slightly warm with a scoop of vanilla ice cream.

About The Cakery

Baking Happiness,
One Slice at a Time.

The Cakery is a cake and café brand founded by Nesth, bringing together years of experience in the food and beverage industry and a genuine passion for good food.

What started with a simple vision to build a cake brand has grown into a place where people can enjoy great food, delicious flavours and moments worth remembering.

Call Us Follow Us
1+Years of Passion
10,000Happy Customers
20,000Cakes & Treats

While cakes remain at the heart of what we do, there's a lot more to explore at The Cakery. From premium cakes, pastries, brownies and cheesecakes to burgers, pasta, loaded fries, snacks and refreshing beverages, our menu has something for every mood and craving.

We believe good food starts with good ingredients. That's why we pay attention to what goes into every product, making sure quality, taste and presentation always come together.

Our journey began in Pappinisseri, and with the love and support of our customers, we've grown into multiple outlets across Kannur. As we look ahead, we're excited to bring The Cakery to more places across Malabar, while keeping the same quality and experience our customers know and love.

At the end of the day, our goal is simple: to serve food people enjoy, create a place they love coming back to, and make every visit a little more special.

Our Vision

Shaping Malabar's cake and café experience.

To shape the future of the cake and café experience across Malabar, building a trusted and recognised brand that brings people closer through quality, flavour and memorable experiences. We aspire to grow across the region while ensuring that every new outlet carries the same standards, taste and identity that make The Cakery special.

Our Mission

Making every visit feel special.

To create exceptional food experiences through quality ingredients, delicious flavours and thoughtful service. We strive to make every product a reflection of our passion for quality, deliver consistent experiences across all our outlets, and build lasting relationships with our customers through food they love and trust.

Help & information

Frequently Asked Questions

What is The Cakery?

The Cakery is a cake and café brand offering premium cakes, pastries, desserts, snacks, burgers, pasta and a variety of beverages.

Who founded The Cakery?

The Cakery was founded by Nesth, bringing years of experience in the food and beverage industry and a passion for creating a quality-focused cake brand.

Where is The Cakery located?

The Cakery has outlets in Pappinisseri, Taliparamba and Kannur, Kerala.

What can I find on The Cakery's menu?

Our menu includes premium cakes, pastries, brownies, cheesecakes, burgers, pasta, loaded fries, snacks and refreshing beverages.

What makes The Cakery special?

We focus on quality ingredients, delicious flavours, consistent taste and customer satisfaction. Every product is prepared with attention to quality and presentation.

Does The Cakery offer more than cakes?

Yes! Along with our range of cakes and desserts, we offer a variety of savoury food options and beverages, making The Cakery a place to enjoy more than just sweet treats.

What are The Cakery's future expansion plans?

Our vision is to expand across the Malabar region, including Kasaragod, Kannur, Kozhikode, Malappuram and Wayanad, while maintaining the quality and experience our customers love.

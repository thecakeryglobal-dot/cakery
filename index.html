About the cakery

Made for moments worth remembering.

The Cakery began in Kannur with a simple goal to make every celebration more special with fresh, delicious cakes. What started with a love for baking has grown into a place where people can find cakes and treats for every occasion.

From birthday, wedding, and anniversary cakes to custom designs, pastries, brownies, cupcakes, snacks, and more, we have something for every celebration and craving. We use quality ingredients and prepare every product with care. With our multiple delivery vehicles.
